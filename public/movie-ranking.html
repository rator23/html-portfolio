The Best Movies According to Alfred

My top 3 movies of all-time

The Equalizer

Denzel Washington stars as a retired Marine
and intelligence operative Robert McCall in a trilogy
of vigilante action-thriller films directed by
Antoine Fuqua

Inception

Dom Cobb (Leonardo DiCaprio) is a thief with the rare
ability to enter people's dreams and steal their
secrets from their subconscious.

Django Unchained

Two years before the Civil War, Django (Jamie Foxx)
a slave, finds himself accompanying an German unorthodox
bounty hunter (Christoph Waltz) on a mission to
capture the vicious Brittle brothers.
